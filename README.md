# E-Waste MasterGame — 3D Sorting Edition

Built from the existing WOW Edition. Nine 3D-styled object illustrations are included in `images/sorting/` as compressed WebP images. They are **illustrated 3D renders**, not actual photographed site evidence or interactive 3D models.

## Included
- 3 missions / 15 bilingual quiz questions unchanged
- 3 pictured objects after each mission (9 in total)
- Drag and drop or click/tap the three management decisions
- Animated logo, Coupe music, neon cursor, E-Bot 2.0, badges and impact dashboard retained
- Music and effects under `audio/`

## How to run
Open `index.html` in a browser, or upload all files and directories to the root of your GitHub Pages repository. Do not omit `images/sorting` or `audio`. Test the full three-mission journey in the browser.

## Educational disclaimer
These are fictional scenarios with specific item conditions. They do not constitute regulatory classification, disposal authorization, or proof of actual environmental outcomes. The existing 15 quiz questions are examples until verified against the user's research spreadsheet.


## Waste-bin visual edition
Three SVG bin illustrations in images/bins replace the sorting answer text-only buttons. Click/tap bins or drag item cards onto them. Category labels remain visible in BM/English; the existing scoring and 9 sorting items are unchanged.


## Applause Celebration (NEW)
At the final result, a quiz score of 250–300 out of 300 triggers a 4.2-second synthesized applause, animated bilingual congratulations and temporary background-music ducking. Scores below 250 retain the normal victory sound. Applause respects SFX ON/OFF and volume. The 9 sorting decisions are not added to the 300 quiz points.
